Advertising

Luxury Brand AR Campaign

Credits

Luxury Fashion House, Creative Agency: Bright Studio

Role

AR Experience Lead

Tools

Spark AR, 8th Wall, Three.js, Substance Painter

Overview

This campaign brought a luxury fashion house’s new collection to life through augmented reality. Customers could virtually try on accessories, explore product details, and share their experiences on social media.

Challenge

Luxury retail depends on tactile experience—the weight of a watch, the texture of leather, the way light plays across precious metals. How do you translate these qualities to a digital medium without diminishing the brand’s prestige?

Solution

We developed an AR experience that prioritized material fidelity above all else. Using photogrammetry and physically-based rendering, we recreated products with unprecedented accuracy:

  • Sub-millimeter geometric detail captured from physical pieces
  • Custom shaders replicating the optical properties of gold, leather, and gemstones
  • Environment-aware lighting that responds to the user’s actual surroundings

Experience Flow

Discovery

Users encounter the AR experience through social media, print ads with embedded triggers, or the brand’s mobile app.

Try-On

Using face and body tracking, products appear realistically positioned and scaled. Users can rotate, zoom, and examine details.

Customize

For configurable products, users explore different materials, colors, and personalization options in real-time.

Share

One-tap sharing creates polished content for social media, with subtle branding that extends campaign reach.

Results

The campaign achieved 2.3 million AR interactions in its first month, with an average session duration of 4.2 minutes. Conversion rates for users who engaged with AR were 3x higher than standard e-commerce.
